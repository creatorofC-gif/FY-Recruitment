DISPATCH #01 // MISSION

Turning Ideas Into Scalable Ventures on Campus.

BloomBox is the official Entrepreneurship Cell of K.J. Somaiya School of Engineering. We are a passionate, student-run body dedicated to igniting the entrepreneurial spirit across the university.

From high-stakes hackathons, venture pitch days, and masterclasses to fostering live college-born startups, BloomBox is where ambitious engineers transform into founders, leaders, and strategists.
